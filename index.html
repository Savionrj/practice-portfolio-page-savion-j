Archaeopteryx
One of the most iconic transitional fossils ever discovered, showcasing features of both dinosaurs and modern birds. With feathered wings, sharp teeth, and a long bony tail, this Jurassic creature helps scientists understand the evolution of flight. It lived around 150 million years ago and
has sparked debates about what truly defines a bird.

Dino Facts

  • Its feathers were asymmetrical, like modern birds.
  • Fewer than 15 fossils have ever been found.
  • Its name means “ancient wing” in Greek.
